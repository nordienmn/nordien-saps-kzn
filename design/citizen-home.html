Before you start

This notice is given under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

  • What we collect: only what you type, upload or choose to share, such as a photo or your approximate location.
  • Why: so SAPS can respond to your report. It may be used as evidence.
  • Who sees it: authorised SAPS officers only. Abuse reports and anonymous tips are restricted, and every access is logged.
  • Your choice: you can report anonymously. We do not need an account.
This is a demonstration. Nothing you enter here is sent to a real police officer.
